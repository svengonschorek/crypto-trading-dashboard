ANALYSIS METHODOLOGY:

**Market Structure:**
- H1 bias determines the overall trend direction
- CHOCH = Change of Character (break of previous structure, potential reversal)
- MSS = Market Structure Shift (confirmed trend change with pullback)
- BOS = Break of Structure (continuation of trend)
- A structure is "confirmed" only after price creates a new opposing swing point

**Order Blocks:**
- Demand Block (DB): Last down candle before strong bullish move (at least 2:1 move)
- Supply Block (SB): Last up candle before strong bearish move (at least 2:1 move)
- "Strength" rating based on: volume spike, wick rejection, subsequent price movement, time since formation
- Only include untested or partially tested blocks within 5% of current price
- "tested" = true if price touched but didn't close through the block

**Liquidity:**
- Swing High: Local peak with lower highs on both sides (minimum 3-5 candles)
- Swing Low: Local trough with higher lows on both sides (minimum 3-5 candles)
- "swept" = true if price moved through level with wicks but closed back inside
- "significance" = high (major swing on H1), medium (clear swing on M15), low (minor swing on M5)

**Fair Value Gaps (FVG):**
- Bullish FVG: Gap between candle[i-1].high and candle[i+1].low (3-candle pattern)
- Bearish FVG: Gap between candle[i-1].low and candle[i+1].high (3-candle pattern)
- Only include unfilled or partially filled gaps within 3% of current price
- "significance" based on gap size relative to ATR and timeframe where it formed

**Chart Patterns:**
- Only include patterns that are at least 70% complete or fully formed
- Measure based on clear swing points, not minor fluctuations
- Target price calculated using standard pattern measurement rules

**Trading Setups - Confidence Scoring (0-100):**
Base score starts at 50, then adjust:
- H1 and M15 bias aligned: +15
- Trading into fresh order block: +10
- FVG confluence with entry zone: +10
- Liquidity sweep before entry: +10
- Clear chart pattern confirmation: +10
- Against H1 bias: -20
- Order block already tested multiple times: -10
- No clear invalidation level: -15

Generate 2-3 DISTINCT setups with different entry approaches:

Setup 1 - "Aggressive/Early Entry":
- Entry at the FIRST potential reversal zone (nearest untested order block or FVG)
- Higher risk but earlier entry
- Tighter stop loss
- Lower confidence score (60-75)
- Best for: Scalping, catching early reversals

Setup 2 - "Conservative/High Probability":
- Entry at a DEEPER level with more confluence (HTF order block, multiple FVG confluence, key support/resistance)
- May require waiting for price to pull back further
- Wider stop but higher R/R
- Higher confidence score (75-90)
- Best for: Swing trades, strong confirmation

Setup 3 - "Breakout/Momentum" (only if applicable):
- Entry AFTER a key level breaks and retests
- Entry zone is ABOVE current price (for longs) or BELOW (for shorts)
- Requires patience for price to move away first
- Moderate confidence (65-80)
- Best for: Trend continuation plays

CRITICAL RULES:
- Entry zones must differ by at least 1.5% in price
- If only one clear setup exists, provide only that one (don't force 3 setups)
- Label each setup with its style: "aggressive_entry" | "conservative_entry" | "breakout_retest"

Risk/Reward: Only suggest setups with minimum 3:1 R/R
